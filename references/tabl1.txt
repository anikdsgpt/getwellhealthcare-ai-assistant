What This Hackathon Is About
This is a week-long, self‑paced hackathon designed to help you think and build like an AI Generalist.
An AI Generalist is someone who can:
Identify a real problem in their work or industry
Decide where AI adds leverage (and where it doesn’t)
Design a simple but effective AI system (prompts, workflows, tools)
Ship an end-to-end usable solution by leveraging any of the AI tools you have learned with us so far
This is not about flashy demos or complex tech. It’s about clear thinking, practical impact, and smart use of AI.
How the Hackathon Works
The hackathon lasts for a week. (Submission deadline: 15th March, 11 pm)
You will think about and pick a real-world idea and build on your own during the week
You submit your project at the end of the week
The intro call explains the format. The real work happens after.
What You Need to Build
A working AI‑powered solution. You can leverage tools like N8N, Emergent, Gemini Studio, make.com, etc.
The solution you build should leverage AI, and the problem statement should be very realistic and real-world.
Evaluation Criteria (100 Points)
1. Problem Framing – 25 pts
Is the problem real, specific, and relevant to professionals/students/businesses/freelancers (can be a problem from any profession or domain)?
2. AI Leverage & System Design – 30 pts
Is AI used thoughtfully? Does it add real leverage?
3. Practical Usefulness – 20 pts
Does this save time, reduce effort, or improve outcomes?
4. Execution Quality – 15 pts
Does it work end‑to‑end? Is it usable? (You need to submit a video/screen recording that shows that your solution is working end-to-end.)
5. Clarity of Explanation – 10 pts
Can you clearly explain what you built and why it matters?
We reward clarity and usefulness, not complexity.
