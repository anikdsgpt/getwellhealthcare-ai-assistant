# Get Well Healthcare - Comprehensive Policies and FAQs

## APPOINTMENTS

### Booking Appointments

**How do I book an appointment?**
We offer multiple convenient booking options:
- **AI Chatbot**: Available 24/7 on our website with instant responses and real-time doctor availability
- **Phone**: Call 1800-123-4567 between 8 AM - 8 PM
- **Walk-in**: Visit our reception desk during hospital hours (8 AM - 8 PM)
- **Mobile App**: Download Get Well Healthcare app from Play Store/App Store (coming soon)
- **Patient Portal**: Log in to your account at www.getwellhealthcare.com/portal

All appointments are confirmed instantly via SMS, email, and WhatsApp with appointment ID, doctor details, and location information.

**How far in advance can I book?**
You can book appointments up to 30 days in advance. For urgent medical needs, same-day and next-day appointments are available based on doctor availability. Emergency cases are prioritized and accommodated immediately.

**What information do I need to book?**
To book an appointment, please provide:
- Full name (as per ID)
- Contact number (for SMS/WhatsApp confirmation)
- Email address (optional but recommended)
- Reason for visit or symptoms
- Preferred date and time
- Any specific doctor preference
- Previous visit history (if applicable)

**Can I book for someone else?**
Yes, you can book appointments for family members or dependents. Please provide their details and your relationship. For minors, parent/guardian information is mandatory.

**Do you offer video consultations?**
Yes, we offer telemedicine consultations for follow-ups, prescription refills, and non-emergency cases. Video consultation fees are 30% lower than in-person visits. Available for select specialties.

### Cancellation and Rescheduling

**Can I cancel or reschedule my appointment?**
Yes, we understand plans change. Our cancellation policy:
- **More than 24 hours before**: Free cancellation, full refund if paid
- **12-24 hours before**: ₹100 rescheduling fee
- **4-12 hours before**: ₹200 cancellation fee, 50% refund
- **Less than 4 hours**: ₹300 cancellation fee, no refund
- **No-show without notice**: ₹500 fee, may affect future booking priority

To cancel or reschedule:
- Use our AI chatbot (fastest)
- Call 1800-BE10X-CARE
- Email appointments@getwellhealthcare.com with Appointment ID
- Visit reception desk

**How many times can I reschedule?**
You can reschedule up to 2 times for the same appointment. Third reschedule requires rebooking with new appointment ID.

**What if the doctor cancels?**
If a doctor is unavailable due to emergency or unforeseen circumstances:
- You'll be notified immediately via SMS/call
- We'll offer alternative time slots with the same doctor
- Or book with another specialist in the same field
- Full refund if you choose to cancel
- Priority booking for your next appointment

### Appointment Day

**What if I'm running late?**
Please inform us immediately by calling 1800-BE10X-CARE:
- **Under 15 minutes late**: We'll accommodate you with minimal wait
- **15-30 minutes late**: You may need to wait for the next available slot (usually 30-45 min)
- **30-60 minutes late**: Appointment may be rescheduled to end of the day or next available slot
- **Over 60 minutes late**: Appointment will be cancelled, rescheduling fee applies

We recommend arriving 15 minutes early to complete registration and paperwork.

**What happens during my first visit?**
For first-time patients:
1. Registration at front desk (10 minutes)
2. Medical history form completion
3. Vital signs check (BP, temperature, weight)
4. Waiting area until doctor is ready
5. Consultation (15-30 minutes depending on specialty)
6. Prescription and follow-up instructions
7. Payment and next appointment booking (if needed)

Total time: Approximately 45-60 minutes

**Can I bring someone with me?**
Yes, one attendant is allowed in the consultation room. For pediatric, geriatric, or special needs patients, attendants are encouraged. Additional attendants can wait in the waiting area.

### Specialist Referrals

**Do I need a referral to see a specialist?**
- **General Physician**: No referral needed, direct booking available
- **Specialists** (Cardiologist, Neurologist, etc.): Referral recommended but not mandatory
- **Super-specialists** (Oncologist, Neurosurgeon): Referral strongly recommended for proper case evaluation
- **Insurance patients**: Most insurance companies require referral from General Physician for specialist visits

Our AI assistant can help you determine if you need a specialist based on your symptoms and guide you to the appropriate doctor.

**How do I get a referral?**
Book an appointment with a General Physician who will:
- Evaluate your condition
- Provide referral letter if specialist care is needed
- Recommend specific specialist based on your case
- Share your medical records with the specialist

Referral validity: 30 days from issue date

### What Constitutes an Emergency?

**Seek immediate emergency care for:**

**Cardiac Emergencies:**
- Chest pain or pressure lasting more than 5 minutes
- Pain radiating to arm, jaw, neck, or back
- Shortness of breath with chest discomfort
- Sudden severe heartburn with sweating
- Irregular or rapid heartbeat with dizziness

**Neurological Emergencies:**
- Sudden severe headache ("worst headache of life")
- Stroke symptoms: Face drooping, arm weakness, speech difficulty
- Sudden confusion or trouble understanding
- Sudden vision problems in one or both eyes
- Sudden trouble walking, dizziness, loss of balance
- Seizures (especially first-time or prolonged)
- Loss of consciousness

**Respiratory Emergencies:**
- Severe difficulty breathing
- Choking or inability to speak
- Blue lips or face
- Severe asthma attack not responding to inhaler
- Suspected pneumonia with high fever

**Trauma & Injuries:**
- Severe bleeding that won't stop
- Deep wounds or cuts requiring stitches
- Suspected broken bones or dislocations
- Severe head injury
- Burns covering large area or on face/hands
- Eye injuries
- Animal bites (especially dog/snake)

**Other Critical Conditions:**
- Severe allergic reactions (anaphylaxis)
- Poisoning or drug overdose
- Severe abdominal pain
- Vomiting blood or blood in stool
- Pregnancy complications (bleeding, severe pain)
- High fever (>103°F) with confusion
- Suicidal thoughts or behavior
- Severe dehydration

### Emergency Department Process

**What happens when I arrive at ER?**

**Step 1: Triage (Immediate)**
- Nurse assesses severity of condition
- Vital signs checked (BP, pulse, temperature, oxygen)
- Priority assigned based on medical urgency:
  - **Red (Critical)**: Immediate life-threatening - Seen immediately
  - **Orange (Emergency)**: Serious but stable - Within 10 minutes
  - **Yellow (Urgent)**: Needs prompt care - Within 30 minutes
  - **Green (Non-urgent)**: Can wait - Within 60 minutes
  - **Blue (Minor)**: Non-emergency - Consider OPD

**Step 2: Registration (Parallel to treatment)**
- Basic information collected
- If patient critical, treatment starts immediately
- Family member can complete paperwork
- Payment discussion after stabilization

**Step 3: Medical Evaluation**
- Emergency physician examines patient
- Diagnostic tests ordered if needed
- Specialist consultation if required
- Treatment plan developed

**Step 4: Treatment**
- Immediate interventions (IV, oxygen, medications)
- Monitoring in ER observation area
- Surgery if needed
- Admission to hospital if required

**Step 5: Disposition**
- Discharge with instructions if stable
- Admission to appropriate ward/ICU
- Transfer to specialized facility if needed

**Do I need an appointment for emergency?**
No. Walk in directly to Emergency Department. No appointment, no referral needed. Treatment priority based on medical urgency, not arrival time.

**What if I'm not sure it's an emergency?**
When in doubt, come to ER or call 102. Our triage nurses will assess and guide you. Better safe than sorry.
